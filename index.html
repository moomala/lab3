Best Movie I've Seen Lately

Lost in Translation (2003)

The best movie I've seen lately is the 2003 film Lost in Translation.

The movie follows a younger woman (Scarlett Johansson) who feels neglected and disconnected from her husband while accompanying him on a trip to Tokyo, where he's always working, and an older man (Bill Murray) who has grown distant from his own wife and is working as an actor on the other side of the world, in Tokyo, as a means to avoid her.

These two hurting and confused souls are staying in the same hotel and end up connecting on a really deep level, all over the span of a week.

An Unusual Connection

Their relationship is especially unique. It's already a bit odd due to their age gap, but it's very special in the sense that it's not a sexual relationship by any means, nor is it even romantic. Their two hurting hearts simply managed to find the only other person in the world who they felt understood them.

Over the course of the film, they both learn from each other. She learns that relationships are hard and take work, while he learns not to take what he has for granted.

What the Film Teaches

Despite the profound bond that these two share, they part ways at the end of the movie, never to see each other again. The film teaches you that even if a relationship was brief, it can still have a major impact on you, and just because it ended doesn't mean it has to be a bad thing.

"The more you know who you are, and what you want, the less you let things upset you." - Bob Harris
